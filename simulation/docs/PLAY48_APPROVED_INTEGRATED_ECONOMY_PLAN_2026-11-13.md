# PLAY-48 — approved integrated food, work and roads recovery plan

**Decision date:** 2026-11-13. **Owner-authorized scope:** more physically justified field/bakery capacity, variable food-for-work pay (1 or 2 portions), authentic forest hunting and river fishing, named physical roads, broad but non-universal employment. The target is a **13-NPC living economy** with causal events, not 13 automatically employed or unboundedly subsidized NPCs.

**Baseline:** Rules63 candidate [PR #92](https://github.com/daboolgame123-creator/Carlos-RPG/pull/92) @ `b397f40` had full **1696/1696 regression PASSED on Python3.10 +3.13** and **3/3 physical food wage/security tests PASSED on both**, [runs #43402906937](https://github.com/daboolgame123-creator/Carlos-RPG/actions/runs/43402906937) and [#43402901506](https://github.com/daboolgame123-creator/Carlos-RPG/actions/runs/43402901506). Not proof of economic endurance. Prior 168h [PR #91](https://github.com/daboolgame123-creator/Carlos-RPG/pull/91) all 13 reached hunger100 despite food physically remaining. No main release or save migration.

## 1. Invariants that must never be weakened

- Original 13 residents, original 12 copper total, initial owned loaves and initial signed obligations accounted for in every A/B. No money, bread, wildlife, fish, water, fuel, labor or skill appears without an audited source or a finite bootstrap/ecological reproduction model.
- Time-zero inventory changes must be explicitly disclosed as *different physical genesis*, not passed off as equivalent controlled A/B. No forcing NPC movement, decisions, work, buying or sleep to make a result green.
- Roads are **real RouteCreated segments** with duration; no agent may know remote routes, food sites or prey until local survey/observation, witnessed rumor or another existing legal knowledge event.
- Food portion arithmetic: a caught animal/fish may yield more than one edible portion only when the physical input (one finite animal/fish lot), processing tools, time and output recipe are logged. This is genuine conversion, never free mid-world stock.
- Wages of one/two personally owned portions occur only after *contracted and completed physical service*, with strict local payer inventory, one-time receipt, co-presence, safety, anti-double-pay and per-worker throttle. Protect open sales, farmer barter, parcel custody and prepaid contracts. No hunger/busy guard bypass; if food is absent the salary remains unpaid.
- Unemployment is legitimate: some roles can remain without work and may seek genuinely accessible alternatives, funded help or forage, but a permanent mathematically forced starvation trap is NOT an economic success.

## 3. Explicit open economic choices

- Hunting/fishing baseline is a **finite-resource test**, not proven self-reproducing ecology. Fish/game abundance, breeding/replenishment, weather, injuries and seasonal rates need quantitative sustainable supply before declaring seven-day production.
- Agree before final acceptance how much labor is socially essential (target a majority employed, *not every single person*) and whether unemployed residents can access genuinely purchased or earned food by other lawful means. Never use a permanently unemployed starvation victim to fake a lower average hunger.
- Larger output is a measured goal, not a hardcoded x2 multiplier. The physical bottleneck may be water, harvesting travel, delivery fuel, employer cash or buyer eligibility, even if one bakery day reaches 12 loaves.
