Only the genuine debtor's acceptance with both real parties physically present may **fund** principal by subtracting actual preinitialized `coin_copper` from creditor and crediting exactly that amount to debtor; fake lender/borrower/authority must not create or redirect any debt. Late full payment may mark `paid` but must preserve original `defaulted_at`.
