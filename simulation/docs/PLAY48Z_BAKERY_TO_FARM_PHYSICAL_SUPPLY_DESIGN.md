The two real fields send water→farm, grain→bakery, bread→market using `supply.py` canonical seven stops:

`well → farm → bakery → market → bakery → farm → well`

This is a physical loop but **not a fully bidirectional food network**. Existing food cargo is strictly `bakery→market` at leg 2. Return legs 3 and 4 are occupied by **actual customer coin remittances** when the cash-return circuit is configured; no authenticated bakery→farm bread lane presently exists. A profitable merchant with 10 shop loaves cannot lawfully credit farm food for free. Adding another paid person without a genuine personal wage or bread transfer merely shuffles hunger.

## Candidate economic contract

- An **explicitly employer-authorized procurement** must establish who owns a genuine bakery loaf *before* it is removed from the baker's physically completed stock. Options to evaluate separately are (A) farm-owned copper purchases bakery bread paid from previous genuine grain proceeds, or (B) a documented real grain-for-bread labor/procurement agreement. Neither option may make an insolvent farm pay or require a farmer to know remote inventory.
- On an **existing actual bakery→farm road** a real available worker/courier must *pick and carry* that loaf. The original seven-stop **cash return** has priority/occupancy conflicts at leg 4, so a review must either define a distinct, trusted real route/worker or an auditable mutually exclusive return freight booking. It must NEVER override a consigned coin parcel or teleport directly into the farm.
- Arrival transfers custody into **owned FARM stock**, with linked procurement, source recipe, buyer debit, seller credit, dispatch ID, courier ID, road IDs, and exact before/after balances. The farm employer may *then* independently commit one unit to a previously signed **Huda WATER-delivery wage** at that worksite; no unearned privilege. The worker consumes her own food through the regular autonomous eating event.
- Keep market food already listed in **open offers** separate from uncommitted employer inventory, protect pre-signed wages, source fuel and seeds, finite water, genuine rain, bakery labor wages and the original 12 copper. An invalid shortage, duplicate dispatch or exhausted worker must fail closed without modifying world state.
