## Accepted behavior
- **No credit without mutual agreement and real copper:** `LoanProposed` (only actual co-located NPC lender) is a recorded offer and neither moves money nor creates outstanding liability. Pending offer may be withdrawn by lender or declined by prospective borrower, again with both present. `LoanAccepted` by the *actual borrower* checks that both parties are present and that their `coin_copper` balances were explicitly initialized. Exactly `principal` copper is deducted from lender and credited to borrower atomically, recording 4 before/after figures; only then is a debt created.
- **Debt is not wallet balance:** `loan_contracts` retains principal, funded transfer, term, due logical time, repaid total and amount still due, together with status. A debtor can legitimately spend or transfer physical money and travel, but cannot delete or shrink the unpaid obligation. A lender's departure likewise cannot remove the contract. No interest, fee, salary, asset collateral or court decision is fabricated.
- **Real repayment:** `LoanRepaid` requires actual debtor and creditor to meet and physical known copper to be available. Supports partial/full payments, rejects overpayment or wallet overflow and records four before/after balances plus timeliness in immutable event history. `loan_repayments` totals exactly reconcile with each contract's `repaid` and `remaining` state.
- **Default is a recorded event, not magic punishment:** Accepted loan schedules exactly one protected noninterrupting `__loan_due` appointment. Only a correctly ordered trusted clock firing at the precise `due_at` marks an outstanding loan `overdue` and `defaulted_at`; this does not seize money, forgive debt, increase principal, punish reputation or teleport a collector. Early full payment retires the schedule as part of its trusted reducer. Full payment after default changes status to `paid`, while preserving `defaulted_at` in history.
- **No forged deadlines or retroactive due-second rewriting:** Generic `ScheduledEventCreated/Cancelled` cannot fabricate/cancel loan due signals; the tested exact `due_at` boundary allows an on-time repayment **before** the scheduled signal and records a missed deadline after it is actually fired, without retroactively changing previous payments.
- **Controlled information:** The `NPCLoanService.my_loans` local view lists only contracts involving the NPC caller; unrelated NPCs get no automatic knowledge of the obligation. The service and `loan_authorized` are **trusted internal capabilities, not public AuthN/AuthZ**.
- **Independent economic ledger:** Existing 01-J/K assistance obligations, 02-C market trades, and 02-B production events retain separate meaning; informal social promise is not converted into a legal debt or payment.
