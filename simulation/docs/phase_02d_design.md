## Intent, physical meaning and gap
02-C atomically transfers bread and copper in a **completed trade**; 01-J/01-K's `obligations` is specifically a separate social/help request, not enforceable financial loan. 02-D does NOT reinterpret either ledger as a debt or treat a casual promise as paid funds. Add a self-contained **opt-in copper-denominated loan contract**:

1. `LoanProposed` — actual creditor NPC, co-located debtor NPC, fixed positive principal **in pre-existing `coin_copper`** and future relative repayment term in simulated seconds. Record the original proposal (site and term); this is **only an offer**, with **no debt** and no money moving. The creditor may withdraw or the debtor decline while the offer is pending.
2. `LoanAccepted` — actual debtor accepts, and both actual parties must again be physically together at the original location; creditor must have a **known sufficient real copper balance**, debtor must have a **known existing balance** with headroom. One atomic reducer event **moves the principal** from creditor to debtor, records the four before/after balances and acceptance time, starts the **fixed due clock** relative to acceptance and one protected noninterrupting due appointment. The debtor **then owes exactly the principal** — not at proposal, and not more. No interest or fees in this bounded slice.
3. `LoanRepaid` — explicit repayment initiated by debtor while creditor is physically present at the same location; both have known copper, debtor has enough, creditor has capacity, requested amount positive and no more than outstanding. One atomic event moves real copper back, records the four balances, cumulative repaid and remaining. **Partial payments** are supported; full early payment marks paid and removes the due appointment without generic cancellation. Repayment after missed deadline is allowed; the recorded missed deadline remains historical even when fully paid.
4. At exact `due_at`, a genuine `ScheduledEventFired` resolves that specific due appointment. If the debt is still outstanding, it marks `overdue`, records `defaulted_at` and **leaves the debt standing**. There is **no automatic confiscation, magically minted penalties, economic punishment, seizure, interest or cancellation**. Future lawful debt collection is a separate project. The creditor/debtor may move, spend money, sell goods or leave the village: the debt and old ledger survive. No wall-clock progress while session is closed.
5. **Privacy:** local debt viewer for each party returns only their own participation in loans, without globally broadcasting contract status to unrelated NPCs. This is an explicitly trusted local operation, never an API to authorize a real player or untrusted caller.

## Event and persistence contracts
- New `loan_contracts` and `loan_repayments` strict event-projected state collections, command capability `loan_authorized` captured in SQLite and replayed.
- Events: `LoanProposed` (creditor actor), `LoanClosed` (creditor withdraw / debtor decline before signing), `LoanAccepted` (debtor actor, funded transfer), `LoanRepaid` (debtor actor, actual transfer), trusted clock `ScheduledEventFired` (due/default).
- All contract IDs unique bounded printable ASCII, integer copper amounts bounded, no negative/bool/unknown, bounded terms, current co-location and not traveling/working, no self-loans. `coin_copper` must be **explicitly initialized** for both parties (unknown != 0). Absolutely no site-owned loans or magic signing behalf of another NPC in this first slice.
- A due signal's reserved `__loan_due` payload cannot be created or cancelled via generic schedule API; only a legitimate accepted loan opens it, and full payment retires it. The exact time is simulation time. Loan records track `principal/repaid/remaining`, status (`proposed/withdrawn/declined/active/overdue/paid`), proposal/acceptance/due/default/settlement times, disbursement balances. Payments separately store before/after and on-time vs late. Every snapshot validates arithmetic and relationships with payment log.
- **Schema 18 / Rules 25**, from 17/24; genuine predecessor worlds **refused unchanged**, with no migration. All feature operations trusted-only; existing `obligations` and `market_trades` do not implicitly change.
