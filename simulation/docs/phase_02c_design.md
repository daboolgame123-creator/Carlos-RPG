## Problem and physical model
02-B makes **site-owned** bakery bread after elapsed work time, without transferring ownership of workshop stock to the baker. Inventing baker ownership would violate provenance. 02-C connects this output to a **local sale without fabricated goods**:

1. Time-zero trusted bootstrap may configure exactly one `MarketStallConfigured {site_id,keeper_id}` per site. The NPC keeper can place an offer for **site-owned stock**, but title and payment remain at the **site**, never in the keeper's personal pocket. A seller NPC may also post an offer for their **own initialized personal stock**. No authority to seize an unrelated NPC's or another site's goods.
2. `MarketOfferPosted` is a trusted, seller-authored fixed quote at the site: ID, owner (site or NPC), seller/keeper NPC, commodity resource, total units offered, payment resource and unit price, and physical site. A posted price is a seller ask, **not** a claimed objective market equilibrium. `coin_copper` is a named copper-currency balance, explicitly seeded only; a different payment commodity enables direct **barter**, with the same fixed quoted exchange ratio. A posted offer does **not** reserve stock; other real-world consumption may make an older quote unfillable. No goods appear when listing.
3. `MarketTradeExecuted` is an explicit, trusted buyer-authored acceptance of an existing immutable quote, with exact quantity and expected unit price. Both participants must be physically present at the listed site (not traveling), stock owner and buyer must have **known, initialized** commodity and payment balances. A site offer requires a registered keeper present and correct. During the **single event projection**, debit goods from owner, credit buyer; debit buyer's payment resource, credit stock owner. Record all **eight** pre/post ledger values in a permanent `market_trades` record. No credit, external liquidity, seller debt or unilateral creation; recipient overflow and negative balances rejected atomically.
4. An offer has a known remaining quantity and may be `MarketOfferClosed` by its actual seller at the market. Repeated sale ID / stale revision is rejected; a second buyer sees only the new remaining quantity. Offers from remote sites are **not displayed** in the local quote viewer; knowledge does not magically propagate to absent NPCs. No player trade API or NPC trading policy yet.

## Acceptance

Barter of other seeded personal commodities must preserve both resource totals. Concurrency, duplicate ID, fake steward, distant seller/buyer, unknown balance, insufficient amount, stale quote, overflows, wrong price, intentional cancellation, rollback after bad batch element, snapshot/replay and backup/restore, invalid direct event and spoofed privilege, and cross-version fail-closed tests. Historical 7-NPC parity must hold when no market configured. Full Python 3.10 and 3.13 matrix must be green on final SHA.

**Deferred:** automated price formation, supply/demand response, merchant NPC decision policy, bargaining, credit, fraud or breach of contract, taxes, spatial shipping, wages, item condition, personal Carlo inventory transfers, public Auth, genuine P0-reviewed permanent world saves. In this first slice prices are **local posted asks** with grounded provenance, not a simulated self-adjusting price economy.
