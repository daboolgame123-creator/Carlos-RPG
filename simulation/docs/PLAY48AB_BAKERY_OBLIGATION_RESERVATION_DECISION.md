## AB change and no-minting contract

- [Dedicated experimental branch](https://github.com/daboolgame123-creator/Carlos-RPG/tree/experiment/play48ab-bakery-obligation-physical-priority-20261111), tested engine change `8e9f19217c03458e383b13883c57a70106d4f1a6`.
- As soon as a **SIGNED farm grain claim** has been earned from a real farm→bakery delivery and a genuine unquoted bakery bread loaf exists, **all actual bakery→market couriers are temporarily prohibited from taking the last uncommitted loaf**. The bakery retains exactly ONE genuine already-baked loaf as an outstanding local obligation (not a new escrow and not a new inventory unit). Other bread remains available to market normally.
- On the existing bakery→farm road, the signed worker may load that one real loaf into custody, follow the existing route, deliver it to FARM, and only THEN settle one claim. The bread was actually produced and physically debited. Market and farm offers retain their reservations, and the baker cannot load food AND copper on the same leg. No claim/no food means original legacy logic remains byte-for-byte in effect.
- **Important edge case:** A courier who collapses or never returns may leave one real loaf waiting at the bakery, causing a genuine opportunity cost for market customers. This is a problem to measure, not a reason to invent delivery or ignore the claim.
- The signed 1 grain → 1 bread contract remains **only an unapproved generous experiment**, NOT proven economically profitable. Alternative 2:1 conversion and protection of bakery labor material/cash must be separately evaluated before any release.
