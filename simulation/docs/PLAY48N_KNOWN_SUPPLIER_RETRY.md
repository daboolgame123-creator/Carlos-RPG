# PLAY-48N — physically revisiting a known fuel seller after one hour

PLAY48 is still OPEN. PLAY48-M already demonstrated one real wood sale and one real charcoal production job with the same actual finite 12 copper; the kiln's owner cannot pay the burner wage until the smith buys the charcoal. The smith actually visited the kiln before charcoal existed and by default never returns to a first-time unsuccessful supplier.

Experimental opt-in `retry_known_supplier_enabled=True` permits a physically present worker with an actual compound recipe and cash to return to a previously personally visited authentic seller **only after 3600 simulated seconds**, without seeing its present stock or quotes remotely. Its previously observed keeper's occupation identifies the potential supply type. Every return uses a genuinely known physical road; if the fuel is still unavailable, no purchase or wages occur. No fixed-price purchase or fake money is injected. This directly tests whether a completed charcoal batch can reach a live buyer.
